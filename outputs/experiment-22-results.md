## Limits

- SkillCorner and DFL are spent; their numbers are match-grouped cross-validation. PFF-64 was never used for training
  or for any EXPERIMENT-22 choice, but the same 64 matches had already been used to compare v8 with v10 (EXPERIMENT-19)
  and other variants (EXPERIMENTS 20, 21, 23), so it is not a fresh holdout (Codex review, 16 Nov; the registration
  text says "fresh" and is left as hashed). One tournament, one provider, broadcast-derived tracking, and our own truth
  rule over PFF's events.
- The DFL gains (+0.10 to +0.12) match what sequence models already showed there (EXPERIMENT-18 arm A: +0.106 on DFL
  gaps), and v10 then lost on PFF; so DFL size is not evidence of transfer. DFL gaps is simulated ball dropout, not the
  competition's tracking.
- Metrica OOF numbers are diagnostic only (Game 1 was v1's training game; Games 2-3 are spent test sets).
- M changes several things at once; A1 and A2 isolate only the blend and the Metrica data. A2 is as good as M off
  Metrica, so the PFF gain is not from the Metrica games.
- A pass here is against v8; the competition truth is derived from corrected tracking, and none of these sources is
  that.
